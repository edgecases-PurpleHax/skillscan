# ADA Compliance

## Overview

Guides ADA compliance audits for digital properties (websites, apps, PDFs) and physical
facilities. Covers WCAG 2.1 Level AA requirements, ADA Title II/III obligations, and
actionable remediation steps.

## Instructions

When conducting an ADA compliance review:

1. **Determine scope** -- ask: Digital (website/app), physical (facility), or both?
   Title II (government) or Title III (private business)?
2. **For digital audits**, evaluate against the four WCAG principles (POUR):
   - **Perceivable** -- alt text, captions, contrast, reflow, text spacing
   - **Operable** -- keyboard access, no traps, timing, navigation
   - **Understandable** -- language, predictability, error handling, labels
   - **Robust** -- valid HTML, ARIA, status messages
3. **Prioritize findings** -- critical (blocks access), major (significant barrier), minor
4. **Note deadline context** -- Title II per DOJ 2024 final rule:
   April 24, 2026 (50K+ pop) / April 26, 2027 (under 50K and special district governments)

## Key Context

- **Title II**: State and local governments (all sizes, no exemptions)
- **Title III**: Businesses open to the public (15+ employees)
- **Section 508**: Federal agencies -- WCAG 2.0 AA

## Penalties
- First Title III violation: up to $75,000
- Subsequent violations: up to $150,000

## Common Pitfalls
- Relying solely on automated tools (miss 60-70% of issues)
- Using accessibility overlay widgets (25% of 2024 lawsuits targeted overlay users)
- Assuming older facilities are "grandfathered" (they are not)
